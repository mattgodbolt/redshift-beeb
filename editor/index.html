    REDSHIFT level editor

    Make rooms for REDSHIFT, a BBC Micro game. Your work stays in this browser until you download it.

    Sending in your rooms

    Press Download rooms.txt. Then either commit it as rooms/rooms.txt in your copy of github.com/mattgodbolt/redshift-beeb and open a pull request, or send the file to Matt. Anything under Problems would stop the game building, so fix those first if you can, but you can download at any time.

    Painting

    • Pick a tile on the left, then drag on the room to paint it. The empty tile rubs out.
    • Right-click the room, or point at it and press I, to pick up the tile there.
    • Rectangle fills the box you drag.

    The two phases

    In the game, SHIFT flips between the Red Phase and the Blue Phase: here, press F or use the buttons. Every colour has a Red Phase value and a Blue Phase one. Tiles and enemies that belong to the other phase show as dotted outlines, and Ash walks straight through them.

    Colours and bands

    Tiles are drawn with colours 1 (the main one), 2 (the second) and 3 (highlights). The room's colours set them at the top of the screen, and a band changes one of them from a line down. Click beside the view to add a band at that line, and drag its marker to move it. A room has at most 8 bands, on lines 2 to 175, each at least 2 lines below the one before. A colour's Blue Phase partner is its opposite (red and blue, yellow and cyan, magenta and green) unless you choose another.

    Things, and the start

    With Things, pick a kind on the left, click the room to place it, and drag it to move it. Delete removes the selected one. An enemy's arg of 1 turns it to face left. An exit is the cage: it takes Ash down (by the room's down exit) to the next sector.

    With Start, click where Ash's feet go: Ash stands on the row below. The first room needs a start, where a new game begins; in another room, it's where the cage brings Ash in.

    Rooms and sectors

    New, Duplicate, Rename id and Delete are above the view. Set the exits and the sector (1 to 5) on the right: a room without a sector is in the same one as the room before it. The map under the view shows how the rooms join, with each one's sector. Click a room there to go to it.

    Keys

    I or right-clickpick up the tile under the pointer
    Fflip the phase
    Ggrid on and off
    [ ]the room before or after
    Ctrl+Z, Ctrl+Shift+Zundo, redo
    Deleteremove the selected thing
