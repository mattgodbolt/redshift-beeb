# REDSHIFT: the music under play. (music/instruments.txt says how a score
# is written.)
#
# It has to sit under gunfire for a long session, so it's slow, quiet and
# mostly space: a pump on the roots and fifths, a lone tune that comes and
# goes, and now and then a drip. The bass is on channel 2, which nothing
# else uses; the tune on 1, which an enemy's shot borrows for a moment; the
# drips on 0, which Ash's own shots take.
#
# It follows the phase. The score is the Red Phase's, in E Phrygian, slow
# and low on a soft lead. In the Blue every degree but E and B goes up a
# semitone into E Lydian, the bass and the tune go up an octave, the tune
# rings on a bell, and it all moves a third faster: the Blue is the short
# half, thin and quick. Its chords change with it: Em F G C Dm in the Red
# are E F# G#m C#m D#m in the Blue, the fifths kept. The drips are only
# ever E and B, which are the same in both.
#
#   rise (4 bars)     the pump alone: Em | Em | F | Em
#   call (4)          the tune: Em | Em | G F | Em
#   thirds (4)        C | Dm | Em | Em
#   climb (4)         C | Dm | F | Em, ending on the theme's fall
#   breathe (4)       drips over a slower pump: Em | F | Em | Dm
#   high (4)          G | F | Em | Em
#   thirds (4)        again
#   ebb (4)           F | F | Em | Em, and round again
#
# In the Red, 94 crotchets a minute (a row is a semiquaver, 8 frames); in
# the Blue, 125 (6 frames).
