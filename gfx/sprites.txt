# REDSHIFT's sprites, facing right where they face at all.
#
#   sprite <name> <width> <height>      then <height> lines of <width> pixels
#
# Flags: "mirror" if it faces a way (it's stored flipped too); "phase" if
# it belongs to a phase (it gets two ghosts: its whole outline with a sparse
# diagonal fill inside, the fill moved between the two, shown in turn out of
# phase so the ghost shimmers: they're the next two sprites, SPR_<NAME> + 4
# and + 8. A ghost is never white: its colour 3 is drawn as 2, since white
# marks what can hurt or be taken). Widths are multiples of 4
# (one MODE 1 byte). '.' is see-through; '1', '2' and '3' are logical
# colours, so they take the band's colours as a sprite moves down the
# screen. Colour 3 is white everywhere but a liquid's band, and it's what
# marks the things that matter: Ash, the enemies, the pickups. Light falls
# from the top left: 3 is lit, 2 the mid tone, 1 the shadow. A comment is
# a whole line starting with '#'.
